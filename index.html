About

I am currently pursuing my Ph.D. in the Department of Computer and Information Sciences (CIS) at the University of Delaware (UD), advised by Prof. Guangmo Tong. My research primarily revolves around Machine Learning, Computer Vision, Combinatorial Optimization and Large Language Models. Specifically, I am dedicated to exploring the application of learning-based and vision-based methods on Combinatorial Optimization. Additionally, my research also involves developing set function learning methods.

Prior to joining the University of Delaware, I completed my B.Sc. degree at the Central South University advised by Prof. Hui Liu.
